# Get started

## A-RAiA Approval Process&#x20;

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Application&#x20;

You express an interest in A-RAiA by applying online.&#x20;
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### A-RAiA Review&#x20;

Assenteo conducts a review based on the standard to verify your compliance.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Register

We’ll add you to our public register to show your _undergoing A-RAiA_.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Remediation Phase

We provide the needed policy and technical support to bring your company up to the required standard.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### A-RAiA Approved

Once the changes are made, we add you to our _completed A-RAIA_ list and you receive a badge. \
\
(An independent audit is coming soon.)&#x20;
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
