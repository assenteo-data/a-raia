# Why trust A-RAiA as a legal team?

When it comes to contracting, what we really need is alignment. \
\
A-RAiA educates and brings a startup to the necessary compliance to begin enterprise consideration, through **deterministic data protection rules**. <br>

1. **SOC 2 reassurance but for contracting requirements** \
   \
   For professionals that review startups’ data processing practices, these standards focus predominantly on security not actual data usage. \
   \
   A-RAIA focuses on how customer data is collected, processed and seen by the startup. Through 21 controls, you can measure which startups to trust and where more reassurance is needed.



2. **Faster clarity on how a startup uses customer data** \
   \
   Review a company with confidence that it meets standardised data usage language. We have built A-RAIA with controls focused on Transparency so you gain the information you need from the startup. \
   <br>
3. **Benchmarked against actual industry standards and relevant ISO 42001, EU AI Act requirements**\
   \
   All A-RAIA controls are mapped against another requirement or industry standard.&#x20;



4. **Built with you, for you**\
   \
   We check A-RAIA quarterly with a group of data protection, legal and industry experts who contribute to the framework.
