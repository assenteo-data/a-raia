# A.2 AI system working transparency

Help customers understand how your AI system works: where the training data comes from, the logic behind it, and how bias is handled.

| Control # | Control                                                                                                                                | Source                                                    | Primary owner    | Supporting functions              |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------- |
| A.2.i     | Share which models you use (your own and third-party), their versions and their providers.                                             | EU AI Act – Technical documentation Article 11 (Annex IV) | Engineering      | AI/ML, Legal & Privacy            |
| A.2.ii    | Give customers enough information about the system to use it properly and in line with the law.                                        | EU AI Act – Technical documentation Article 11 (Annex IV) | Product          | Customer Success, Legal & Privacy |
| A.2.iii   | Keep a record of system versions, changes and releases.                                                                                | EU AI Act – Technical documentation Article 11 (Annex IV) | Engineering      | Product                           |
| A.2.iv    | Document what input data the system needs, the quality and performance to expect, and the conditions it works best in.                 | EU AI Act – Instructions to deployers Article 13          | AI/ML            | Product, Engineering              |
| A.2.v     | Build in features that let people oversee the system and understand its outputs (human oversight).                                     | EU AI Act – Instructions to deployers Article 13          | Product          | Engineering                       |
| A.2.vi    | Help customers set up their own human oversight and monitoring.                                                                        | Art 26 – Deployer obligations                             | Customer Success | Product                           |
| A.2.vii   | Give customers the integration and configuration guidance they need to use the system safely and compliantly for its intended purpose. | EU AI Act – Instructions to deployers Article 13          | Customer Success | Engineering, Legal & Privacy      |
