# C.1 Data protection principles

The company shall process data in the AI system with industry-standard data protection principles. the controls in place to minimize data risk

<table><thead><tr><th width="109.609375">Control #</th><th width="380.22265625">Control</th><th>Source</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>C.1.i</td><td>The company has assessed data classification levels, including personal data and company data.</td><td>Enterprise vendor requirement</td></tr><tr><td>C.1.ii</td><td>The company has implemented personal data minimisation and purpose limitation principles, to ensure only relevant personal data is collected and processed.</td><td>Enterprise vendor requirement</td></tr><tr><td>C.1.iii</td><td>The company has implemented safeguards to minimise personal data in training and testing datasets where feasible.</td><td>Art 10 – Data and data governance</td></tr><tr><td>C.1.iv</td><td>The company has defined, documented, and implemented retention schedules, including deletion or anonymisation when personal data processing is no longer required.</td><td>Enterprise vendor requirement</td></tr><tr><td>C.1.v</td><td>The company has implemented and documented anonymisation and/or pseudonymisation techniques appropriate to the processing risks.</td><td>Enterprise vendor requirement</td></tr><tr><td>C.1.vi</td><td>The company provides processes and technical measures to support data subject rights requests (e.g., access, deletion, objection).</td><td>Enterprise vendor requirement</td></tr></tbody></table>
