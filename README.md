# Introducing A-RAiA

**A-RAIA is the data usage and processing protocol for AI-native companies.** It structures your data flows, AI usage, and privacy posture into deterministic outputs that legal and DPO teams can review and trust.

We’re rethinking the standards for the enterprise adoption of early-stage AI, reducing unnecessary compliance debt and ensuring free flow of new technologies. **Thanks for joining us!**&#x20;

### Why we exist

Globally, SOC 2 and ISO standards are currently the gold standard that unlock enterprise doors for B2B software companies.&#x20;

For companies that build on top of existing LLM models however, these standards are only part of the story. They do not help procurement teams understand how AI systems process customer data.&#x20;

AI startups therefore struggle to gain enterprise trust, often relying on reputation growing over time, personal networks for introductions, or paying the bill for expensive certification routes - all just to get a foot in the door. &#x20;

### Say hello to A-RAiA 👋&#x20;

We’ve built a framework that benefits both sides: startups gain credibility, and enterprises gain a trusted, efficient way to onboard AI vendors.&#x20;

**A-RAIA is Assenteo’s data processing and usage protocol for AI systems.**&#x20;

We aspire to give AI credibility by making governance efforts visible. To do this, companies that are part of A-RAIA meet a set of 21 controls. This is a standard that legal and privacy teams need to see, when it comes to how the startup processes customer data. A-RAIA is:

* **Practical and meaningful governance.** Rooted in real-world needs.
* **Enterprise adoption-ready.** Lightweight enough for you to adopt fast; resilient enough for your trust.
* **Community driven.** You have a seat at the table in shaping A-RAiA.

By standardising privacy and AI governance expectations, we aspire to bridge the trust gap between innovative startups and risk-conscious enterprises.

### A-RAiA's Controls&#x20;

Through 21 controls, A-RAIA consolidates what legal and privacy teams need to see. As a result, you can close an enterprise client faster, and comply with standards users trust.
