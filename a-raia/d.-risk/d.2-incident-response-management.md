# D.2 Incident response management

Have a plan to spot, assess and deal with AI incidents and malfunctions.

| Control # | Control                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Source                                       | Primary owner    | Supporting functions                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| D.2.i     | Have a process to detect, assess and manage AI incidents and malfunctions.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Art 73 – Incident reporting                  | Engineering      | Security, AI/ML                                |
| D.2.ii    | Define incident severity levels and who to escalate the incident to.                                                                                                                                                                                         | Art 73 – Incident reporting                  | Security         | Engineering, Leadership                        |
| D.2.iii   | Document incidents, their impact and what steps you took after the incident occurred.                                                                                                                                                                        | Art 73 – Incident reporting                  | Engineering      | Security                                       |
| D.2.iv    | Tell affected customers and stakeholders about significant incidents, where appropriate.                                                                                                                                                                     | Art 73 – Incident reporting                  | Customer Success | Legal & Privacy, Leadership                    |
| D.2.v     | Fix the root cause after an incident and take steps to stop it happening again (corrective and preventive action).                                                                                                                                           | Art 73 – Incident reporting                  | Engineering      | AI/ML, Security                                |
| D.2.vi    | Set and share incident response timelines for each severity level (how quickly you'll acknowledge, contain and notify customers), in line with your contracts and the law (e.g. the GDPR 72-hour breach notification, EU AI Act serious-incident reporting). | Art 73 – Incident reporting; GDPR Article 33 | Security         | Engineering, Customer Success, Legal & Privacy |
