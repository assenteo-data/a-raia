# A.2 AI system working transparency

The company shall provide any relevant and necessary information and explanations regarding the working of the AI system (traceability of the Training Data, the logic behind the AI System, mitigation of bias etc.).

Explanation: \
The Buyer should be able to understand how the AI System works.&#x20;
